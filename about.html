About Domain Marketing

A free, independent toolkit for people selling domain names.

Why this site exists

Most people who own a spare domain name do nothing with it, or park it and hope. Advertising income from parked names fell away through 2025 and 2026, and what is left is the plain fact that a domain earns money when someone sells it well. Selling well is a set of small skills: pricing, choosing a venue, building a page, writing to a buyer, closing safely. None of them is hard. Most are badly explained, and the explanations that exist are usually written by a company with something to sell you.

Domain Marketing is a free toolkit and a set of playbooks for those skills. It is paid for by advertising, sponsorship and reader contributions, not by commissions on your sale.

How we work

  • Published figures only. Every fee in the venue table and the calculator comes from the company’s own page, with the date we read it. Where a company does not publish a rate where we could read it, we say so instead of guessing.
  • Sources under every playbook. If a claim came from somewhere, the link is at the bottom of the page.
  • Tools that show their working. The scorecard lists its factors and weights. The calculators show the sums. Estimates are labelled as estimates.
  • No dark patterns. No invented urgency, no fake counters, no pop-ups that hide the page.

What it is built on

Before writing a line, we read the public pages of 39 companies and publications in the domain industry: marketplaces, registrars, brokers, escrow and financing services, portfolio platforms, trade news sites, forums and learning resources. The tools exist because that reading showed a gap. The industry is full of marketplaces and news; it is short of neutral, practical help for the person doing the selling.

How the site stays current

The market wire and the video library refresh automatically every day from publishers’ own feeds. The venue fees and reference figures are reviewed by hand each quarter, and each carries its read date.

Independence

The tools are free and we take no cut of any sale you make with them. If we ever offer a paid service, or earn a referral fee for an introduction, it will be labelled where it appears. Sponsors are labelled and have no say over the content.
