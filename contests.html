Contests and prizes

Three contests that reward the skills this site teaches. Free to enter, judged on skill, and the best entries are published so everyone learns from them.

  • The 90-word pitch
    Entries close Jan 31, 2027     The brief. Write the first outbound email for a domain you own or an invented one. Ninety words or fewer. It must say what you own, why it fits the reader, how a sale would work, and give an easy way to say no.
    Judged on. Clarity, honesty and whether a busy founder would reply.
    The prize. The winning email is published with credit on this page and featured on the home page for a month. A cash prize is added whenever a sponsor or reader contributions fund one, and is announced here before entries close.
  • For-sale page of the season
    Entries close Feb 28, 2027     The brief. Build a for-sale page for a domain you own, with the builder on this site or by hand. Send the live address.
    Judged on. Does a first-time visitor on a phone understand it is for sale, see how to act, and trust the page, inside five seconds.
    The prize. The winning page is featured in the for-sale page playbook with a link and credit. A cash prize is added whenever a sponsor or reader contributions fund one, and is announced here before entries close.
  • Name the startup
    Entries close Mar 31, 2027     The brief. We describe an invented company in two sentences on this page each season. Propose one unregistered or owned-by-you domain for it, and one sentence on why.
    Judged on. The phone test, length, and fit with the brief. Names that lean on an existing trademark are disqualified.
    The prize. The winning name and its author are credited on this page. A cash prize is added whenever a sponsor or reader contributions fund one, and is announced here before entries close.

This season’s startup to name: A two-person company that helps independent bakeries take pre-orders online and plan the morning bake. Friendly, practical, not techy.

Rules

  1. Entry is free. No purchase or contribution is needed, and making one does not improve your chances.
  2. One entry per person per contest per round. Entries must be your own work.
  3. These are contests of skill. Entries are judged by the editors against the criteria stated for each contest. The judges’ decision is final.
  4. Entries that use another party’s trademark, make false claims or contain unlawful content are disqualified.
  5. You keep ownership of your entry and allow us to publish it on this site with credit.
  6. Non-cash prizes are as described. A cash prize applies only where one has been announced on this page before the closing date. Winners are responsible for any tax on a prize.
  7. Open to adults wherever such contests are lawful. Void where prohibited.
  8. We use your email only to run the contest and contact winners.
