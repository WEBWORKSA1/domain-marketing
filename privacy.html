Privacy and cookies

Last updated November 26, 2026.

The short version

  • The tools run in your browser. What you type into a calculator or builder is not sent to us.
  • If you submit a form, what you wrote is emailed to the team through a form-delivery service.
  • This site shows advertising from Google AdSense, which uses cookies.
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not run our own analytics or tracking scripts.

Forms

When you send a form (a plan request, a contact message, a contest entry, a pledge or an application), the fields you filled in are delivered to the team’s inbox by FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service. The message also includes the address of the page you sent it from and the time. Lead forms attach a one-line summary of the last name you scored and the plan you built, so that the reply can be useful. We use what you send to answer you and for the purpose stated on the form, and we do not pass your details to anyone else without asking you first. We keep correspondence for as long as it is needed for that purpose and delete it on request.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings, and you can opt out of many vendors’ use of cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info.

The cookie notice on your first visit lets you choose “Essential only”. In that case we ask Google to serve non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the button below.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may also be shown a consent message supplied by Google when one is configured for this site.

What is stored in your browser

We keep a few small items in your browser’s local storage so the site behaves as you left it: light or dark theme, your cookie choice, and whether you have already sent a lead form. The last name you scored and the last plan you built are kept only until you close the tab. None of this is sent to us unless you submit a form.

Video

Videos are shown as still images until you press play. Pressing play loads the player from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain, and YouTube may then set its own cookies under its own policy.

Links to other sites

Headlines in the market wire, the venue table and the sources under each playbook link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what we hold about you from a form you sent, ask us to correct it or delete it, or withdraw from any contact. Use the contact form and we will act on it. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

This site is written for adults running a business or a portfolio. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
