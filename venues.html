Where to sell a domain

15 routes to a sale, with what each is best at and the fees each company publishes. Read on Nov 26, 2026. Companies change their terms, so confirm before you list.

VenueBest forPublished feesSource
Sedo
Marketplace		 The widest international reach. Priced names are also offered through its registrar partner network.10% when the name has a buy-now price and is parked or uses Sedo's sales page; 15% for make-offer or auction; 20% when the buyer comes through the partner network. A 3% transfer-only service exists for deals you close yourself.Sedo 'Buy, Park, Sell' page; DomainGang's breakdown of Sedo's own fee explanation
Afternic
Marketplace network		 Puts priced names in front of people searching at partner registrars, with in-house brokers and a lead centre for inquiries.No commission figure is stated on the brokerage page we read. Check the current fee schedule in your account before you list.Afternic domain brokerage page
Saw.com
Marketplace and brokerage		 Self-serve for-sale pages with live chat and lease-to-own built in, with brokers available when a deal needs one.9% self-service; 15% full-service brokerage.Saw.com seller page
Dynadot
Registrar marketplace		 Low-friction listings and auctions for names already held at the registrar, plus expired-name auctions for buyers.10% on user listings and auctions; 5% when the sale completes through a Pay Link. Inbound brokerage 10% (minimum $50); outbound brokerage for ultra-premium names 15%.Dynadot aftermarket page and brokerage help article
Namecheap Market
Registrar marketplace		 Budget and mid-priced names listed straight from a Namecheap account in a few clicks.Flat 10% per transaction. Asking prices from $5 to $1,000,000.Namecheap knowledge-base article on listing a domain
Spaceship SellerHub
Registrar marketplace		 Sellers who want modern for-sale pages, lease-to-own and names imported from any registrar.10% per sale; 5% on checkout and self-serve links, according to the SellerHub FAQ text. The page itself did not load for us on the read date, so confirm on the site.Spaceship SellerHub FAQ text as shown in search results
Flippa
Marketplace		 Names with a story: an old site, traffic or revenue attached. Buyers there shop for businesses as well as names.Listing packages from $29, plus a success fee of 3% to 10% depending on the value of the asset.Flippa domains page
Atom
Curated brandable marketplace		 Coined, brandable names. Accepted listings are shown at a fixed price with logo files, and many carry a payment plan.No seller commission is stated on the page we read. Check the seller terms before you submit names.Atom marketplace listing page (formerly Squadhelp)
BrandBucket
Curated brandable marketplace		 Startup-style brandables sold at a fixed price with a sample logo. Listings we saw ran from about $1,500 to $8,400.Seller terms are not stated on the page we read.BrandBucket home page
An independent broker
Brokerage		 High-value names where finding and persuading one specific buyer is the whole job.Usually a percentage of the sale price, commonly 10% to 20%, and almost always with an exclusivity period.DomainInvesting.com guide to domain brokers
Escrow.com
Escrow for direct deals		 Closing a sale you found yourself. The buyer pays the escrow company, the name moves, then you are paid.2.6% (minimum $50) on domains up to $5,000, falling in steps to 0.7% above $10 million. The concierge service starts at 5.2% (minimum $100).Escrow.com domains page
Your own for-sale page
Direct		 Keeps the buyer relationship and the margin with you. You do the marketing; an escrow service handles the money.No commission. Budget for the escrow fee on the deal.Build one with the for-sale page builder on this site
Investor communities
Investor-to-investor		 Moving names quickly to other investors at wholesale prices, using standard sale templates and trader ratings.Expect investor pricing, a fraction of what an end user would pay.NamePros domain auctions guide
DomainEasy
Financing		 Offering a buyer monthly payments. It publishes three structures: lease-to-own for $1,000 to $50,000 names, a deposit plan for $50,000 to $500,000, and a balloon plan above that.Seller pricing is managed in the seller dashboard and is not listed on the financing page.DomainEasy financing page
Efty
Portfolio platform		 Investors who want their own branded storefront, for-sale pages and portfolio tracking in one place.No pricing is shown on the product page we read.Efty Investor product page

How this table is kept honest
