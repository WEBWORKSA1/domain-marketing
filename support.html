Keep the tools free

Domain Marketing has no paywall and takes no cut of your sale. Advertising covers part of the cost. Reader contributions and sponsors cover the rest.

Where contributions go

  • Keeping the site running. Hosting, the domain, and the time it takes to re-check every published fee each quarter.
  • Promotion and marketing. Getting the tools in front of people selling their first name, who are the ones most likely to be underpaid or scammed.
  • Hiring talent. Paying writers, developers and video makers for specific pieces of work.
  • Contests and prizes. Funding cash prizes for the contests, which otherwise run with non-cash prizes.

How giving works

Fill in the pledge form. We email you a secure payment link for the amount you chose, and nothing is charged until you use it. You can direct the money to one of the four uses above or leave the choice to us.

Domain Marketing is not a registered charity. Contributions are not tax-deductible and are not an investment; they are support for a free resource.

Other ways to help

  • Send a correction when a fee in the venue table changes.
  • Enter a contest, or fund a prize.
  • Share a tool with someone selling their first name.
  • If you run a business in this industry, sponsor a tool or a playbook.
