Advertise, sponsor or partner

The people who use this site are in the middle of selling or buying a domain name. If your service helps them do that, here is how to reach them.

What you can sponsor

  • A toolYour name beside one calculator or builder, labelled as its sponsor, for a season.
  • A playbookA labelled sponsor line on one guide. You do not see or change the text.
  • A contest prizeYou fund the cash prize and are named as its sponsor on the contest and the results.
  • The market wireA labelled sponsor line above the daily headlines.

What sponsorship does not buy

Inclusion, position or wording in the venue table and the calculator; a say in what a playbook recommends; or readers’ contact details. Those rules are why the audience trusts the site, which is what makes a placement here worth having.
