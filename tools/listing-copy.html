Draft your listing and social posts

One form, six drafts: a short marketplace summary, a full description, taglines for the for-sale page, two social posts and a forum listing.

Making the drafts yours

The drafts are deliberately plain: the name, who it suits, three facts and how to buy. Read each one aloud and cut anything you would not say to a buyer in person. Replace the bracketed parts of the forum listing with your real price, registrar and expiry date.

Where each one goes

  • Summary and description: the listing fields on a marketplace.
  • Taglines: the one line under the name on your for-sale page.
  • Social posts: from an account that says who you are. Lead with the use, not the asset.
  • Forum listing: investor communities, at investor prices.

The playbook on marketing in public covers which audience pays which price.

Questions people ask

Can I use the text as it is?

Yes, it is yours to use and change. You will sell better if you edit it into your own voice.

Why no hashtags or emoji?

Because the people who buy domains for businesses respond to clear sentences. Add them if your audience expects them.

Why does it avoid claims about value or traffic?

Because a claim you cannot support on request costs you the sale and your reputation. State facts about the name and let the buyer judge.
