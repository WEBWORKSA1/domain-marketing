How the score is built

The scorecard reads the structure of the name and nothing else. Five factors, each scored out of 100, are combined with fixed weights:

FactorWeightWhat it checks
Clarity30%One dictionary word, two words that read cleanly, a pronounceable coined name, or a string nobody can read aloud
Length25%Characters before the dot. Shorter scores higher; the fall is steep past ten
Extension20%.com first, then the extensions with their own demand, then everything else
Buyer intent15%Whether a word sits in a category where companies spend heavily to win customers
Clean typing10%Hyphens and mixed digits, which cost visits every time the name is said aloud

The price bands come from the score and the kind of name, using a fixed curve. The investor band is a small fraction of the marketplace band, because an investor has to leave room to resell. The end-user band is a multiple of it, because a company with a specific use will pay more than a stranger browsing.

What it cannot see

Traffic, age, backlinks, past use, live trademarks, and above all real sales of similar names. Those can move a price by ten times in either direction. Treat the band as the first line of your pricing notes, then check it against comparable sales.

Questions people ask

Is this an appraisal?

No. It is a rule-of-thumb reading of the name’s structure, meant to give you a band to test and a list of what would lift the result. An appraisal weighs comparable sales and facts this tool cannot see.

Why does my name score lower than another tool says it is worth?

Tools differ in method and in what data they hold. This one deliberately uses no sales data, so it is conservative on names whose value comes from an active trend, and generous to none.

Does the tool store the names I type?

No. Scoring runs in your browser. A name is only sent anywhere if you choose to submit one of the forms on this site.

What should I do with a low score?

Check whether the name has something the score cannot see, such as traffic or an obvious buyer. If it does not, run the drop test in the portfolio calculator before the next renewal.
