Reading the table

Each row is one route to a sale: a venue, and the way the buyer reached you there. On tiered marketplaces the same name can cost 10%, 15% or 20% depending on whether it was priced, where it pointed and whether the buyer came through a partner. The table shows every published tier, so you can see what the fee would be in the best and worst case before you list.

Two rows are for sales you find yourself and close through an escrow service. On deals above $5,000 the published escrow percentage steps down, so the calculator marks those figures as a ceiling.

Fee is not the whole decision

A venue that charges twice as much can still be the better choice if it is the one that finds the buyer. Use the table to understand the cost of each route, then choose on reach. The where-to-sell playbook matches kinds of name to routes, and the venue table lists the source for every rate.

Questions people ask

Where do the rates come from?

From each company’s own published pages, read on the date shown on the Where to sell page. Venues whose rate was not stated on the page we read are left out of the calculator instead of guessed.

Why is a venue I use missing?

Either its rate was not published where we could read it, or we have not reviewed it yet. Enter the rate from your own account in the “add your own rate” box to include it.

Does the buyer or the seller pay escrow?

Whoever you agree. The calculator assumes the seller pays, which is the conservative case.
